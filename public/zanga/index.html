Self-hosted reading client

Thousand Zen Reader Sect

A focused reader for organizing, saving, and reading manga from a Suwayomi bridge you control.

Support Privacy Policy

What it does

  • Connects to your own Suwayomi bridge.
  • Lets you browse installed sources and search your catalogs.
  • Saves titles, reading progress, and reader preferences.
  • Supports offline chapter downloads and cached metadata.
  • Syncs library and reader settings through your private iCloud account.

Content library

Thousand Zen Reader Sect does not include, host, sell, or provide a catalog of books or comics. You configure the bridge and sources you are permitted to use.
