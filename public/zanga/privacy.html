Privacy Policy

Thousand Zen Reader Sect

Last updated April 29, 2026

Overview Support

Summary

Thousand Zen Reader Sect is designed as a client for a bridge that you configure. The app does not create developer-run accounts, does not include advertising SDKs, and does not sell personal information.

Data Stored On Your Device

The app stores reading-related data locally, including:

  • Saved titles and reading progress.
  • Reader preferences such as reading mode and page sizing.
  • Favorite sources, repository settings, and bridge URL settings.
  • Downloaded chapters and cached source metadata for offline use.

iCloud Sync

If iCloud is available for your Apple ID, the app may use Apple's iCloud key-value storage to sync library, favorites, progress, pinned sources, repository settings, and reader settings across your devices. The bridge URL remains local to each device.

Network Connections

The app connects to the Suwayomi bridge URL that you configure. That bridge may connect to sources you choose outside the app. Those requests are controlled by your bridge configuration and are not operated by the app developer.

Developer Access

The developer does not receive your library, reading progress, downloaded chapters, bridge URL, or source credentials through a developer-operated service.
