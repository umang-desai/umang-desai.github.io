Overview Privacy Policy

Contact

For support, email umangd03@gmail.com. Include your device model, OS version, app version, bridge URL host, and a short description of what happened.

Bridge Setup

Thousand Zen Reader Sect requires a Suwayomi bridge that you run or control. On Mac and Simulator, the default bridge URL is http://127.0.0.1:4567. On iPhone or iPad, use the LAN address of the Mac or server running your bridge.

Troubleshooting

  • If the bridge does not connect, confirm the bridge is running and reachable from the device.
  • If a source is empty, refresh the bridge and confirm that the source extension is installed and working on the bridge.
  • If images do not load, check your network connection and whether the bridge can fetch the chapter pages.
  • If offline reading is missing pages, redownload the chapter while the bridge is online.

Content

The app does not include a built-in content catalog. Use only sources and material that you have the right to access.
